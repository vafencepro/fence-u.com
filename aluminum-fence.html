Is aluminum fence pool-code compliant?

Ultra Aluminum builds pool-style panels designed around the common barrier requirements — minimum height, limited gap spacing at the bottom, and picket spacing that rejects a 4-inch sphere — and we quote those styles specifically for pool enclosure work. Barrier codes are enforced locally, so the inspector and the local amendment always have the final word: tell us the jurisdiction when you quote and we’ll supply the panel style and self-closing gate hardware that matches what your inspector expects to see.

Aluminum or ornamental steel — which should you quote?

Aluminum wins on corrosion: it doesn’t rust, which makes it the default for pool decks, coastal exposure, and anywhere sprinklers hit the fence line every morning. It’s also lighter to handle and faster to set. Ornamental steel earns its place where impact strength and security presence drive the spec — heavier commercial frontage and institutional perimeters. We supply both; compare the ornamental steel line and quote whichever the job calls for, or split the property between the two.

What finishes and warranties come with Ultra Aluminum?

Panels and posts come powder-coated in the standard color range (black leads every order book, with bronze, white, and other colors by style), and the finish is backed by Ultra Aluminum’s manufacturer warranty passed through to the project. Warranty terms and finish documentation live in our resource library, ready to hand your customer with the closeout package.

What drives wholesale aluminum fence pricing?

Grade is the big lever — residential, commercial, and industrial sections step up in metal and price — followed by height, style (flat top, spear, pool), color, and gate count. Aluminum tracks the metals market like steel does, so account pricing is quoted current, per project. Estate gates and custom fabrication price separately through the metal shop, quoted from your dimensions.

What does a complete aluminum order include?

Panels by style and height, posts by function — line, corner, end, and gate, each with the right cap — plus walk and drive gates with hinges and latches rated for the panel weight. Non-standard openings and estate entrances route through our custom gate shop. Orders ship as complete systems against your take-off, coordinated across Virginia and North Carolina through your account rep.
